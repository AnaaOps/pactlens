RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Residential Rental Agreement is made on 01 April 2025 between the Landlord and the Tenant for the premises at Flat 4B, Green Park Extension, New Delhi.

1. Parties
The Landlord is Sharma Properties Pvt Ltd. The Tenant is Ayesha Khan.

2. Premises
The Tenant shall occupy Flat 4B, Green Park Extension, New Delhi, including one parking slot.

3. Term
The lease term is 11 months commencing 01 April 2025 and ending 28 February 2026.

4. Monthly Rent
The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of ₹28,000 (Rupees Twenty Eight Thousand only) on or before the 5th day of each month.

5. Security Deposit
The Tenant has paid a security deposit of ₹56,000. The Landlord shall refund the security deposit within 30 days of the Tenant vacating the premises, after deducting unpaid dues and documented damages beyond normal wear and tear.

6. Notice Period
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days written notice. During the first 3 months (lock-in), early termination by the Tenant attracts one month rent as fee.

7. Maintenance and Repairs
The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs and major maintenance of plumbing and electrical systems. The Tenant shall keep the premises in clean condition and pay for minor consumables.

8. Liability
Each party shall be liable for loss arising from its own negligence. The Tenant is not liable for structural defects existing prior to possession.

9. Subletting
The Tenant shall not sublet the premises without prior written consent of the Landlord.

10. Dispute Resolution
Disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of courts in New Delhi.

11. Renewal
Upon expiry, renewal shall be by mutual written consent on revised terms, if any. There is no automatic renewal.
