RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT (REVISED)

This Residential Rental Agreement is made on 01 April 2026 between the Landlord and the Tenant for the premises at Flat 4B, Green Park Extension, New Delhi.

1. Parties
The Landlord is Sharma Properties Pvt Ltd. The Tenant is Ayesha Khan.

2. Premises
The Tenant shall occupy Flat 4B, Green Park Extension, New Delhi, including one parking slot.

3. Term
The lease term is 11 months commencing 01 April 2026 and ending 28 February 2027.

4. Monthly Rent
The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of ₹32,000 (Rupees Thirty Two Thousand only) on or before the 5th day of each month.

5. Security Deposit
The Tenant has paid a security deposit of ₹64,000. The Landlord shall refund the security deposit within 90 days of the Tenant vacating the premises, after deducting unpaid dues and documented damages beyond normal wear and tear.

6. Notice Period
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 60 days written notice. During the first 6 months (lock-in), early termination by the Tenant attracts two months rent as fee.

7. Maintenance and Repairs
The Tenant shall be responsible for all maintenance and repairs including plumbing and electrical systems, except structural collapse. The Landlord bears no obligation for day-to-day upkeep.

8. Liability
The Tenant shall be solely liable for any loss, injury, or claim arising from use of the premises and shall indemnify and hold harmless the Landlord from all claims.

9. Subletting
The Tenant shall not sublet the premises without prior written consent of the Landlord.

10. Dispute Resolution
All disputes shall be referred to binding arbitration in New Delhi under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Courts shall have limited jurisdiction.

11. Auto-Renewal
This Agreement shall auto-renew for successive 11-month terms unless the Tenant provides written cancellation notice at least 45 days before the then-current term ends.

12. Unilateral Amendments
The Landlord may modify these terms at any time at its sole discretion by email notice to the Tenant.
